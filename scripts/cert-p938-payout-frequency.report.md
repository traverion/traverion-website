# Payout frequency pricing

Production: https://partner.traverion.com/partner/money?tab=settings

The supplier’s active agreement is 15%. Monthly shows 15% with no additional fee. Twice a month shows the same 15% plus 3%, total 18%.

Both cards were selectable. Saving monthly kept that choice after reload, then the original twice-a-month preference and the minimum payout were restored. The agreement commission, connected account, verification, and payout gates were unchanged. Gates stayed off. No payout was sent.

11/11 PASS on desktop and mobile. Chunk `SupplierEarnings-38zLw-L4.js`.
