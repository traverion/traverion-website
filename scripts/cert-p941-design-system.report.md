# Partner design system pass

Production: https://partner.traverion.com

Income & Payouts and Analytics now use one tab control: same height, type, selected underline, hover, and arrow-key movement. Their page titles are both 25px Plus Jakarta Sans at desktop weight 700.

Bookings, Inbox, and Listings search fields are 40px with an 8px radius and 13px type. Toast motion is 180ms in and 160ms out. Reduced motion still removes those transitions.

6/6 PASS on desktop and mobile. No horizontal overflow on the checked pages.

Still separate by design: the calendar view switch, listing category chips, and the home schedule toggle are compact filters, not page tabs. Listing editors, onboarding steps, and booking drawers keep their existing workflows.
